Partnership Customers

Customer performance for the selected period

Customer Points ⓘ Current loyalty points. ↕ Tier ⓘ Active loyalty tier for the current quarter. Running Tier ⓘ Customer's tier based on their Total Paid for the selected quarter or date range. Previous Tier ⓘ Customer's tier based on Total Paid during the previous quarter or previous date range.

Example: If Q3 is selected, Previous Tier is based on the customer's Total Paid in Q2. 		Next Tier ⓘ The next partnership tier above the customer's Running Tier based on Total Paid for the selected period. Status ⓘ Compares "Tier" column with "Running Tier" column for the selected period. ↕ Status II ⓘ Compares "Previous Tier" column with "Running Tier" column for the selected period. ↕ Total Paid ⓘ Sum of paid transactions only. Excludes payments made using Shopify Store Credit and Gift Cards. ↕ Store Credit ⓘ Total amount paid using Shopify Store Credit. ↕ Gift Card ⓘ Total amount paid using Gift Cards. ↕ Order Total ⓘ Sum of all order totals. ↕ AOV ⓘ Average Order Value. Calculated as Order Total divided by the number of Orders. Spend to Upgrade ⓘ Remaining spend required to reach the next partnership tier based on Total Paid. Orders ⓘ Total number of orders placed by the customer. ↕ Progress
