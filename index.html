How Total Paid is calculated

Total Paid only includes payments from orders that were created and paid within the selected period.

✓
Order was created within the selected period
✓
Order was paid within the selected period
✕
Gift Card payments are excluded
✕
Store Credit payments are excluded

Tier

The customer's active loyalty tier for the current quarter.

Running Tier

The tier calculated from the customer's Total Paid for the selected period.

Previous Tier

The customer's tier based on Total Paid during the previous reporting period.

Status II

Compares the customer's Previous Tier with their Running Tier to determine whether they are Upgrading, Maintained, or Downgrading.

Status

Compares the customer's Present Tier (Tier column) with the Running Tier column for the selected period.

Points

Displays the customer's current loyalty points.

AOV

Average Order Value, calculated as Order Total ÷ Orders.

Spend to Upgrade

The remaining amount required to reach the next partnership tier based on Total Paid.
